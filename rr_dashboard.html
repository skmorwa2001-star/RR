Analysis Framework

How we identified the most reliable players under pressure from raw ball-by-ball data.

📦 Data Pipeline
1️⃣
Raw Data Ingestion
Loaded 6 CSV files: ball_by_ball (200K+ rows), matches, competitions, teams, playing_eleven, player_mapping
2️⃣
T20 Filtering
Isolated 883 T20 matches across IPL 2022–2026 and international T20Is via competition format flags
3️⃣
Pressure Zone
Filtered to overid ≥ 16 — the death overs where match outcomes are decided. 44,454 pressure deliveries extracted.
4️⃣
Score Computation
Computed Bat Score = Avg×SR/100; Bowl Score = WktPerInn÷(Econ/6); Reliability blends avg, 15+% and win%
5️⃣
Minimum Thresholds
10 innings min (global T20), 5 innings (IPL-specific), 5 innings (chase finisher). Eliminates small-sample noise.
6️⃣
Shortlisting
Cross-referenced scores with RR's playing style, overseas slot budget, age curve, and IPL 2027 availability
🏏 Batting Metrics
Avg Death Runs — Mean runs per innings in overs 16–20
Death SR — Strike rate in overs 16–20 (runs/balls × 100)
Bat Pressure Score — Avg × SR ÷ 100 (combined hitting value)
15+% / 20+% — % of innings the batter scored 15+ / 20+ in death overs
Reliability Score — 40% Avg + 40% (15+%) + 20% Win%
Chase Finisher Avg — Death-over runs in Innings 2, chase ≥ 150
🎳 Bowling Metrics
Death Economy — Runs per over in overs 16–20
Wkts/Inn — Mean wickets per death-over spell
Bowl Score — Wkts/Inn ÷ (Economy/6) — higher = better
Dot% — % of death-over balls that were dot balls
Wkt% — % of innings in which at least 1 wicket was taken
Reliability — (12−Econ)×0.4 + Wkt%×0.4 + Win%×0.2
